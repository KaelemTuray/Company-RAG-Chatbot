# Northwind Outfitters Refund Policy (2026)

## Standard returns
Customers may return unused items within 45 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be in original packaging with tags attached.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse. Banks may take an additional 3 to 7 days to post the credit.

## Non-returnable items
- Custom-engraved products
- Gift cards
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale"
- Opened tents or sleeping bags that show signs of use

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free for defective items. For all other returns, a flat $7.95 return label fee is deducted from the refund.

## Damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged, contact support within 14 days with a photo. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund, including original shipping costs.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or color are free and ship within 2 business days of receiving the original item.
