# Trailhead 3 Tent: Product FAQ

## Specs
- Capacity: 3 people
- Weight: 5 lb 11 oz (packed weight 6 lb 4 oz)
- Floor dimensions: 88 x 62 inches
- Peak height: 44 inches
- Season rating: 3-season

## How waterproof is it?
The rainfly has a waterproof rating of 3,000 mm and the floor is rated 5,000 mm. Taped seams come standard.

## Can I use it in winter?
No. The Trailhead 3 is rated for 3 seasons (spring, summer, fall) and is not designed for heavy snow load. For winter camping, see the Summit 4 tent.

## How do I clean it?
Spot clean with mild soap and lukewarm water. Do not machine wash or use bleach. Dry fully before storing to prevent mildew.

## What is included in the box?
Tent body, rainfly, 9 stakes, 3 pole segments, guylines, and a stuff sack.

## Warranty
The Trailhead 3 carries a 2-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear, UV damage, and accidents are not covered.

## Setup time
Most people can pitch it in about 8 minutes with two people.
