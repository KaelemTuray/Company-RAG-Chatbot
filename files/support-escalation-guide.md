# Customer Support Escalation Guide

## Tier 1 (front-line agents)
Handle order status, returns within policy, shipping questions, and product FAQs. Target first response time: 4 business hours.

## When to escalate to Tier 2
Escalate if the customer:
- Requests a refund outside the 45-day return window
- Reports a safety issue with a product
- Has contacted support three or more times about the same issue
- Disputes a charge with their bank

Tier 2 agents can approve exceptions up to $150.

## When to escalate to a manager
- Refund exceptions above $150
- Legal threats or media inquiries
- Suspected fraud
- Any injury report

Managers respond within 1 business day.

## Safety issues
Any report of injury or fire must be logged in the Incident Tracker the same day and sent to the Product Safety team at safety@northwind.example. Do not offer compensation before Product Safety reviews the case.

## Tone guidelines
Apologize once, clearly, then move to solutions. Avoid blaming the customer or the carrier.
