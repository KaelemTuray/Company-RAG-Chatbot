# Northwind Outfitters Shipping Policy (2026)

## Shipping options (United States)
| Method | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5 to 7 business days | $5.99, free on orders over $75 |
| Expedited | 2 to 3 business days | $14.99 |
| Overnight | 1 business day | $29.99 |

## Processing time
Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern ship the same business day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM ship the next business day. We do not ship on weekends or federal holidays.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada and the United Kingdom only. International orders take 8 to 14 business days. Customers are responsible for duties and import taxes.

## Restricted destinations
We cannot ship camping fuel canisters or bear spray by air, so these items are not available for Expedited or Overnight shipping, and are not shipped internationally.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed once the order ships. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear.

## Lost packages
If a package shows no movement for 7 days, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation or reship the order.
