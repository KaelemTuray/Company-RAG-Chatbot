# Northwind Outfitters Employee Time Off Policy

## Paid time off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue PTO based on tenure:
- Years 0 to 2: 15 days per year
- Years 3 to 5: 20 days per year
- Years 6 and up: 25 days per year

Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Any days above 5 expire on December 31.

## Requesting time off
Requests of 3 days or fewer need 1 week of notice. Requests longer than 3 days need 3 weeks of notice. Submit requests in the HR portal, and your manager will respond within 2 business days.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 8 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Parental leave
Birth and adoptive parents receive 12 weeks of paid leave at full salary. Leave must begin within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## Company holidays
The company observes 10 paid holidays, including a company-wide shutdown from December 25 to January 1.

## Blackout period
PTO is generally not approved between November 20 and December 20 for retail and warehouse staff because of the holiday rush.
